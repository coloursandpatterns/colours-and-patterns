Raajas India Private Limited is a leading manufacturer and wholesale supplier of premium kitchen and dining essentials. We produce and supply everything a hotel, restaurant, or retail business needs — from fine cutlery and crockery to heavy-duty commercial cookware and kitchen equipment.

With over 15 years of manufacturing experience and a distribution network spanning all of India, we've become the trusted procurement partner for some of the country's finest hospitality brands. Our commitment: consistent quality, competitive pricing, and reliable delivery — every time.
