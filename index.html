FAQ

Questions
we get often

Everything you want to know about working with Colours & Patterns — answered honestly.

What makes Colours & Patterns different from other agencies?

We are 100% in-house — no freelancers, no outsourcing. Everything from design to performance ads is handled by our core team in Guwahati. We also bring 8+ years of regional market intelligence from Northeast India that national agencies simply don't have.

Which industries do you specialize in?

We have deep experience in automotive, real estate, hospitality, retail, FMCG, healthcare, education, and infrastructure. That said, our strategic approach adapts to any growth-minded business in Northeast India or beyond.

Do you work with businesses outside Guwahati / Assam?

Yes. While our roots are in Guwahati, we serve clients across Assam, all seven Northeast Indian states, and select national brands looking for deep regional penetration. Our WhatsApp-based workflow makes remote collaboration seamless.

What does a typical retainer engagement look like?

Retainers typically cover social media management (content + posting + engagement), performance marketing (Meta & Google), monthly reporting, and a dedicated account manager. We start with a 2-week onboarding sprint before going live.

How long before I see results from digital marketing?

Paid campaigns (Meta/Google) can show measurable results within 2–4 weeks. SEO and organic brand-building typically take 3–6 months for compounding impact. We provide transparent monthly reports so you track progress every step of the way.

How do I get started with Colours & Patterns?

Simply fill in the contact form below or call us at +91 79084 36393. We'll schedule a free discovery call to understand your brand, goals, and budget — then put together a custom proposal within 48 hours.
